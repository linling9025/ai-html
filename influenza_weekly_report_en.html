Public Health Recommendations

📋 Action Items

  1. Vaccination Promotion: Target unvaccinated populations in high-activity states (CA, TX, AZ), with particular focus on healthcare workers and individuals with chronic conditions
  2. Healthcare Resource Alert: Hospitals in Southern and Southwestern regions should prepare for sustained high patient volumes; consider activating surge capacity plans
  3. School Surveillance Enhancement: Strengthen school absenteeism monitoring in high-activity states to identify outbreaks early
  4. Antiviral Stockpiling: Ensure high-risk populations have timely access to oseltamivir and other antiviral medications
  5. Public Education: Intensify respiratory hygiene and home isolation messaging, particularly in areas with high community transmission
